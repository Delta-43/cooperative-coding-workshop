## Left out

- **Eiffel Tower steps (1,665) and Big Ben's Great Bell (13.7 tonnes):** the official pages blocked my fetch (403 and a bot check). Search summaries agree with these numbers, but I didn't read them on the source page, so they are not here.
- **Angel Falls (979 m):** the figure comes from a 1949 survey and is disputed.
- **Great Pyramid original height:** a reconstruction, not a measurement.
- **Challenger Deep, Panama Canal length, Canada's coastline:** the answer depends on who measures.
- **Python's first release (1991) and the UN's 193 member states:** I did not find a primary page that states them in a sentence.

## Notes for the game

- The answers range from 16 to over a million. Score by **relative error** (distance divided by the true answer), not plain distance. Put this scoring rule in the contract.
- Show the unit on the answer screen, and put it in the question text.
- Where a note says "say ...", use that wording. It stops a fair guess from being marked wrong.
- Re-open the pages in the week of the workshop. Most are stable, but the Everest and Burj Khalifa pages are the ones that have changed before.
